The Importance of Sleep

Getting enough sleep is very important for your health. It helps your body recover and stay focused throughout the day. Without proper sleep, we feel tired and cannot perform well in work or studies.

A doctor once said:

Sleep is not a luxury. It is a necessity for the human body and mind.

As one expert says, Good sleep equals good health.

During sleep, our body goes through different stages including REM sleep which is very important for memory.

Experts say we need at least 6 8 hours of sleep every night.
